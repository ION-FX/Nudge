# Classroom guide

A short walkthrough for teachers and students.

## For teachers

### 1. Claim your instance
The first visit to a fresh Nudge redirects to **/setup**. Create your account —
you become the admin teacher. You'll also pick the **teacher invite code** here;
share it only with colleagues. Students never need it.

### 2. Create a class
Dashboard → **+ New class**. Each class gets a 6-character **join code**, shown
prominently on the class page. If a code leaks (e.g. it ends up in a group chat
for the wrong school), hit **↻ Regenerate**.

### 3. Upload materials
**+ Material** accepts a file (`.pdf`, `.txt`, `.md`, `.csv`, `.json`, `.py`,
images) or pasted text. Anything with readable text is tagged **"✦ Nudge can
read this"** — that's what lets the AI co-pilot tutor from your actual
readings. Slides exported as PDF work great.

### 4. Post assignments and quizzes
**+ Assignment** takes instructions, an optional due date, and points. For
quizzes, build questions one at a time (multiple choice, true/false, short
answer) or paste CSV rows. Quizzes start as drafts — hit **Publish** when
you're ready; students get notified.

> Tip: after posting an assignment, use **✦ Generate practice questions with
> AI** to give students a no-stakes warm-up that targets the same concepts.

### 5. Grade
Open an assignment to see every submission: text, attached file, late flag.
Enter points and feedback inline. Students see the grade and your feedback
immediately, and it shows up in their notifications, in **My grades**, and in
the **Gradebook** (with CSV export for your SIS or spreadsheet).

### 6. Watch the activity feed
The class page lists the latest uploads, assignments, quizzes, and
announcements, plus the full roster. Click a student's name for their complete
record in this class.

## For students

### 1. Join
Your teacher gives you a code. Dashboard → **+ Join a class**.

### 2. Do the work
Assignments accept typed text, a file, or both. You can resubmit until you're
happy — but resubmitting clears a grade, and anything after the deadline is
marked **late** (it still counts as submitted).

### 3. Get stuck? Ask Nudge
Every assignment, material, quiz, and class has an **✦ Ask Nudge** button. Be
honest about what you don't understand — that's what it's for. It will ask you
questions back, give hints, and point you at the right reading.

It will **not** give you answers, and neither pestering nor trickery will
change that. That's the design: you do the learning, Nudge does the nudging.

### 4. Keep track
- **🔔 Notifications** — new assignments, grades, announcements, quizzes.
- **My grades** — every score and piece of feedback, per class.
- **Calendar** — everything due, across all your classes, sorted.

## Frequently asked

**Can I use Nudge during a quiz?** No. Quizzes are one attempt and the tutor
won't help you answer them — it will happily explain concepts *after*.

**Does Nudge store my assignment if I only ask about it?** Your conversations
are saved so you can pick up where you left off (`/tutor` lists them, and you
can delete them). Teachers don't read your chats.

**What file types can I upload?** PDF, plain text, Markdown, CSV, JSON, Python,
and common images. Text-bearing files are readable by the tutor; images are
stored for download only.

**I forgot my password.** Ask an admin/teacher — password resets by email
aren't in v1. Admins can't see your password either; they can only create a
new account for you.
